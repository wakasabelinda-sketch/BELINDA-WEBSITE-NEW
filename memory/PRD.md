# Belinda Wakasa, Advocate - Legal Services Website

## Original Problem Statement
Design and generate a professional legal services website for Belinda Wakasa, Advocate of the High Court of Kenya.

## User Persona
- **Primary**: Potential clients seeking legal services in Kenya
- **Secondary**: Business entities requiring corporate advisory, litigation, or conveyancing services
- **Location Focus**: Nairobi, Kenya

## Core Requirements
- **Identity**: Belinda Wakasa, Advocate of the High Court of Kenya
- **Theme**: Minimalist, premium legal aesthetic
- **Colors**: White (primary), Black (text), Gold (#C5A059 accents)
- **Typography**: Playfair Display (headings) + Manrope (body)

## What's Been Implemented (December 2025)

### Pages/Sections
1. **Home/Hero** - Value proposition with CTAs (Schedule Consultation, Click-to-call)
2. **About** - Professional profile with stats (10+ years, 500+ cases, 98% satisfaction)
3. **Practice Areas** - Bento grid with Commercial Law, Litigation, Conveyancing, Corporate Advisory
4. **FAQ** - 5 frequently asked questions with Shadcn accordion
5. **Contact** - Form with mailto integration, contact details
6. **Footer** - Navigation, practice areas, contact info
